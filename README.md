## About

Royal City Temporary Agency provides temporary, temp-to-perm, and permanent placement for job seekers. We handle pre-screening, personal interviews, vetting, and software assessments for every candidate.
Celebrating over 5 years, our staffing agency provides successful on-site and remote staffing solutions for your various staffing needs.
Royal City Temporary Agency is a small Women-Owned staffing agency serving Royal City and the surrounding suburbs.We are an experienced firm that builds long-standing beneficial relationships with our clients and candidates.
As strategic career professionals, we use our expertise to impact the lives of our candidates and improve our client’s company culture, one exceptional match at a time.
Our team is the best staffing agency in Royal City to help keep companies running and people working, so reach out and let us know how we can help.

## Services

Recruiters: Admin Staffing, Technical Staffing, and other Staffing Options.

Job Seekers: pre screen interviews, pre qualification tests, resume review, and other resources.

Online application, resume reviews, and downloadable skill tests
